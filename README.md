# SpongebobGame

## Game Overview

Move SpongeBob SquarePants around Bikini Bottom to catch 15 jellyfish before you lose all 3 lives.

- Catch 15 jellyfish to win the game.
- Avoid dropping 3 lives or the game ends.
- Explore Bikini Bottom while chasing jellyfish.

## How to Play

1. Use the arrow keys (or your chosen controls) to move SpongeBob.
2. Catch jellyfish as they appear.
3. Keep your lives above zero.
4. Reach 15 jellyfish to win.

## Win/Lose Conditions

- Win: Collect 15 jellyfish.
- Lose: Drop 3 lives.

## Notes

- This is a fun Bikini Bottom-themed arcade game.
- Be quick and avoid mistakes to catch all the jellyfish!
